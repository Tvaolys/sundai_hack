## SC5: MA ABE/ESOL teachers' DESE/NRS reporting burden

- https://nrs.ed.gov/rt/ma/2024/narrative-report — MA's federal NRS narrative report (PY2024, published). State itself documents reporting friction: employment rates require "local provider survey and data matching with the Department of Unemployment Assistance (DUA) and the state wage interchange system (SWIS)" via SSN file transfer; MA fell short of employment-rate targets 2 years running and argues the data "underrepresent" outcomes (self-employed contractors/housekeepers missed). Also contracts Diploma Sender for credential tracking. Institutional trace of measurement/data-collection pain — at state level, not teacher level. Confidence: verified.
- https://www.doe.mass.edu/acls/laces/ — MA ACLS mandates LACES (LiteracyPro Systems) as the student data management system; NRSWeb snapshot confirms state staff + providers use LACES + GEM$ monitoring. System exists and is vendor-locked (single vendor, LiteracyPro). Confidence: verified.
- https://nrsweb.org/policy-data/state-snapshots/massachusetts — confirms MA follow-up methods (data match + surveys), assessments (MAPT, TABE, BEST Plus, CLAS-E). Confidence: verified.
- https://adultedresource.coabe.org/resources/reporting-adult-education-data/ — COABE national resource on NRS reporting (generic, no MA-specific complaint). Confidence: verified (doc exists; pain not quoted).
- Search for practitioner forum complaints (COABE journal, teacher "data entry" complaints) returned no dated first-person posts — one result was an AI-generated content-farm article (afroliterarymagazine.com, "Updated May 8, 2026") of no evidentiary value. No educator-voice trace captured in 3 phrasings.

VERDICT: WEAK (reporting system and its friction are documented at state level — LACES monopoly, DUA/SWIS matching failures — but zero dated first-person teacher complaints found; teacher-level pain remains unverified.)
