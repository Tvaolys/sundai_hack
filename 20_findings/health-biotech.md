## E2: Small-clinic vaccine cold-chain temperature logging pain (low-resource settings)

- **WHO PQS E006 page (Temperature Monitoring Devices)** — https://extranet.who.int/prequal/immunization-devices/e006-temperature-monitoring-devices — defines the full device taxonomy (thermometers, freeze indicators, electronic recorders, data loggers/EMDs) that PQS-prequalified procurement requires. Shows the regulated device landscape any software-only hack must coexist with. Confidence: verified.
- **WHO Vaccine Management Handbook WHO/IVB/15.04, dated 1 Aug 2015** — https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-IVB-15.04 — official guidance on manual monitoring + "new devices which monitor and log temperatures electronically." Confidence: verified.
- **Cleverlogger explainer (Australia "Strive for 5" guidelines, undated web article)** — https://cleverlogger.com/why-do-vaccine-fridges-need-logging-at-5-minute-intervals/ — confirms the pain protocol: continuous 5-min logger + weekly download + "twice-daily manual min/max readings are still required on the fridge temperature chart — the logger doesn't replace those"; breach assessment demands logger data "not just the hand-written" chart. Evidence the double-burden (manual chart + logger) exists even in well-resourced systems. Confidence: verified (vendor blog, secondary source).
- **Gavi VaccinesWork article (Phase 6 2026–2030 strategy launch, ~2026)** — https://www.gavi.org/vaccineswork/inside-one-africas-most-complex-supply-chains — ColdTrace remote temperature monitoring (Nexleaf Analytics) deployed across **43 countries** 2021–2025, "up to 80% reduction in vaccine losses", US$1.7B of vaccines monitored/yr, 1 in 6 children benefiting; countries (Rwanda, Tanzania, Nigeria) integrating temperature monitoring into national data platforms. Strong signal that the donor-funded monitoring niche is already occupied at national scale. Confidence: verified.
- **Hardware constraint note**: Gavi/Nexleaf ColdTrace and PQS loggers (e.g., Ideabytes IBI-MTH series per PQS spec guide) are purpose-built hardware. A laptop/phone-only build cannot do in-fridge 24/7 sensing without external hardware; a phone-camera OCR of existing paper charts / Fridge-tag readouts could be the only software-only angle. No smartphone-only cold-chain solution surfaced in 3 phrasings.
- Searches tried: (1) WHO PQS manual logbook burden study; (2) "Fridge-tag/30 DTR cost unaffordable"; (3) manual twice-daily logbook errors; (4) smartphone open-source cold chain apps. No first-person practitioner complaint threads found (no posts surfaced from r/globalhealth or EPI forums via these phrasings).

**VERDICT (E2): CROWDED** — Gavi-funded ColdTrace already at scale in 43 countries; twice-daily manual logging persists as a regulatory requirement layered on top of loggers, not as an unsolved gap. Hardware constraint (no in-fridge sensors) further limits a laptops/phones-only build.
